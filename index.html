Capones & the Coves

An island-hopping day from Pundaquit: a Spanish-era lighthouse, then lunch on a cove lined with pine trees. Back at Crystal Beach for your last night on the coast.

Boats go out in the morning when the sea is calmest. Mid-November can still bring rough water, so treat this day as weather-dependent and keep the beach as plan B.

Good to Know

🛂

Entry

US citizens can stay up to 30 days without a visa. Complete the eTravel registration online shortly before arrival.

🚐

Hire a driver

A van with a driver for the Subic and Zambales legs beats buses with luggage. Book a week or two ahead.

🌀

Weather

Late November is the tail end of typhoon season. Get travel insurance and keep boat days flexible.

💵

Cash

Carry pesos for Zambales; small resorts and boatmen often don't take cards.

📱

Connectivity

Get a Globe or Smart eSIM before landing so Grab works the moment you arrive.

🛫

O'Hare transfers

Outbound you'll change terminals by train. On the return, clear customs and recheck bags at ORD.

☀️

Sun

The sun is strong even when it's cloudy. Pack a rash guard, a hat, and reef-safe sunscreen.

🎒

Cove day kit

Water, lunch, a dry bag, and water shoes for the rocky lighthouse trail.
